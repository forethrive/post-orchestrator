# Checked facts

Statistics that someone has opened at the source and checked. Posts may only
use numbers from this file or from `brand/ideas.md`, and every source a post
cites must be listed here: `npm run validate` and the Python agents reject any
other URL.

To add a fact, open the original source (not an article quoting it), copy the
numbers exactly with their context, and add an entry in the same format.
`/generate-posts` in Claude Code adds entries as it researches. Commit new
entries so everyone's generator can use them.

## US small businesses using AI (Federal Reserve survey, 2025)

- Source: AI Adoption and Employment Expectations: Evidence from a Survey of
  Small Business Owners. Liberty Street Economics, Federal Reserve Bank of New
  York, 8 October 2026
- URL: https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/10/ai-adoption-and-employment-expectations-evidence-from-a-survey-of-small-business-owners/
- Checked: 2026-10-13
- About: the Federal Reserve Banks' 2025 Small Business Credit Survey, fielded
  September to November 2025. 5,248 US employer firms (with at least one
  employee) answered the AI questions.
- Facts:
  - 46% of employer firms said their business or employees were using AI tools.
    Another 15% planned to adopt them in the next 12 months.
  - Among firms using AI, 51% had integrated it partly or fully into their
    business processes, and 63% said AI was somewhat or very important to
    production.
  - 31% of respondents reported increased sales from their firm's use of AI.
  - 77% of respondents reported no change in their firm's labour costs from the
    use of AI.
  - Hiring outlook for the next 12 months: AI users had a net expectation of
    higher employment of 33 percentage points, non-users 15. Comparing similar
    firms, the gap stayed at 10 to 13 points.
  - Revenue outlook: among AI users, the share expecting revenue to rise beat
    the share expecting it to fall by 48 points. Among non-users, by 21 points.

## AI assistants in customer support (NBER study, 2023)

- Source: Generative AI at Work. Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li and Lindsey R.
  Raymond, NBER Working Paper 31161, 2023
- URL: https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161
- Checked: 2026-10-13
- About: data from 5,179 customer support agents who were given a generative AI
  assistant at work.
- Facts:
  - Issues resolved per hour rose 14% on average.
  - Novice and low-skilled agents improved by 34%.
  - Experienced and highly skilled agents saw minimal impact.

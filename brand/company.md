## Who we are

- Forethrive is an AI-first technology company based in India, working with
  clients worldwide.
- We pair machine-learning expertise with solid software engineering: one
  partner from idea to production.
- Website: forethrive.ai

## What we do

1. **Custom AI solutions (our specialty):** LLM applications and AI agents,
   assistants that answer from a company's own documents (RAG), generative AI
   integration, model fine-tuning and evaluation, secure on-prem or cloud
   deployment.
2. **Machine learning and analytics:** forecasting, recommendations, BI
   dashboards, data pipelines.
3. **Computer vision and NLP:** quality inspection, document and speech AI,
   semantic search.
4. **Intelligent automation:** process automation, system integrations, AI
   copilots.
5. **Web and mobile development:** React and Next.js apps, iOS and Android,
   SaaS platforms.
6. **Cloud, DevOps and data:** AWS, Azure and GCP, CI/CD and monitoring, data
   engineering.
7. **AI consulting and strategy:** AI readiness audits, proofs of concept, team
   training.

How we work: Discover (goals, data, constraints), Design (architecture, UX,
roadmap), Build (short sprints with working demos), Scale (launch, monitor,
improve).

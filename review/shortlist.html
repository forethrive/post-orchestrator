LinkedIn shortlist

To post one:

  1. Download the image (or use the PNG in the images folder).
  2. On LinkedIn, switch to the Forethrive page and start a post.
  3. Add the image and paste the caption, then post it.
  4. Paste the first comment, which has the link.
Made with Post Studio. Images here are compressed for sharing; full-quality PNGs are in the images folder.

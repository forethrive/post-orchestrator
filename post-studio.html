Forethrive Post Studio

One look for every post. Pick a format, type your text, download the image, then paste it into LinkedIn with the caption format below.

Brand colours

Layout and type rules

Layout

  • Dark navy background with the wave art along the bottom. Never use a white or photo background.
  • Logo and name always top left. Web address always bottom left.
  • 72px margin on every side. Text stays left-aligned.
  • One idea per post: one headline, at most one supporting line.
  • Formats: 1080×1350 portrait for the feed; 1080×1080 square only when needed.

Typography

  • Font: Manrope. Headlines in bold, supporting text in regular.
  • Sentence case everywhere. No all-caps headlines.
  • Headline: 12 words or fewer. Supporting line: 25 words or fewer.
  • Use the blue-to-cyan gradient only on one big number per post.
  • No emojis inside images.

Carousels (multi-slide posts)

  • Slide 1 is the hook: one bold claim or question.
  • Middle slides: one point each, using the same layout.
  • Last slide: one clear action, such as "Message us" or "Visit forethrive.ai".
  • Keep to 5 to 8 slides.

Post types and rhythm

  • Announcement: a launch, news or milestone.
  • AI insight: one useful lesson, tip or myth busted.
  • Stat: one number and what it means.
  • Client win: the problem, what we built, the result.
  • Hiring: the role and how to apply.
  • Suggested rhythm: 3 posts a week, mixing at least one insight and one real project.

Caption format (copy this structure)

Line 1: The hook. One sentence that makes someone stop (under 125 characters, because LinkedIn cuts the rest).

Lines 2 to 5: The value. Short lines, one idea each, plain words, a real example or number.

Last line: One action. A question to answer, or "Message us to talk about your use case."

Sign-off: your name and role.
Hashtags: 3 at most, from #AI #GenerativeAI #Automation #Forethrive
Tag @Forethrive in every post. Add the link forethrive.ai in the first comment, not the caption.

Voice

Do

  • Be specific: name the problem, the tool and the result.
  • Write like talking to one business owner.
  • Show real work, even small demos, with permission.
  • Reply to comments within a day, as the company page or personally.

Don't

  • Don't use buzzwords like "revolutionary", "cutting-edge" or "game-changing".
  • Don't promise results you can't show.
  • Don't share client names, data or screenshots without written permission.
  • Don't change the colours, font or layout, even for special occasions.
